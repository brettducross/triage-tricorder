1. Optical Vitals & CIE Lab Colorimetry

WebRTC 30fps green-channel photoplethysmography (rPPG) for resting HR, HRV (RMSSD), and respiratory rate, plus CIE L*a*b* palpebral conjunctiva erythema (anemia proxy) and scleral b* yellowness index.

2. Digital Stethoscope, Voice & Sonar

Disables browser voice filters (echoCancellation: false) and applies BiquadFilterNode bands for Heart (20–150Hz), Lung (100–1200Hz), and Bowel sounds, plus 5s vocal jitter/shimmer/HNR and 18–22kHz sonar.

3. 6-Axis IMU Cardio & Neuro Lab

Uses 100Hz+ accelerometer & gyroscope telemetry on the sternum for Seismocardiography (SCG) aortic valve timing, outstretched-hand FFT tremor classification (4–12Hz), and 2D Romberg sway quotient.

4. 240Hz Touch & Display Neuro Lab

High-frequency capacitive touch digitizer tests: live radial-deviation Archimedes Spiral drawing test, 10-second alternating two-finger bradykinesia tap test, Critical Flicker Fusion strobe, and Pupil Flash reflex.

Synthesizes calibrated 250–8000Hz pure tones for Left/Right ear threshold screening, paired with a guided forehead Weber vibration/acoustic lateralization test to help clinicians distinguish conductive vs. sensorineural patterns.
